The machine room under the money.

Broc is the team people talk to. Clara reads the numbers. Clive looks for the move. Brox is the floor beneath them that speaks to blockchains.

When on-chain value is part of the job, holding, moving, or earning, Brox has a wallet ready and carries the transaction. Gas, signatures, and the differences between networks stay inside the fabric. Upstairs, the person still sees a balance, a goal, a plain result.

Seed phrases stay off the screen. Wallet popups stay off the screen. The approval they already gave Broc is the one that counts.

Broc is what people see.
Brox is what the chain hears.

02 // OBLIVION

The chain, unspoken

A request leaves Broc as a human intention: move this, save that, fund the goal. Brox turns it into a chain call and turns the receipt back into a figure Clara can explain. The user keeps the conversation they were already having.

03 // FABRIC

Many networks, one surface

New chains plug in downstairs. Clara and Clive keep a single surface. Brox absorbs addresses, fees, and finality so the product people trust remains Broc, even when the money moves on-chain.
